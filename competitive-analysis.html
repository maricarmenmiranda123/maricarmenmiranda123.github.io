What the whole grid says

Benable's corner is small and expensive to enter

Done-for-you reach has few players, and each has a catch: Statusphere starts at $10,000, Stack Influence won't let you pick creators and needs 50 to 100 at a time, Hummingbirds only takes CPG brands in national retail, and minisocial is about $300 a creator.

Managed is often a tier, not a company

JoinBrands managed is $4,000/mo, Insense $4,600/mo for 20 creators, Social Cat $250 per collaboration, HFC from $150K, and Billo and TRIBE offer one without public prices. Self-serve tools are moving right on the grid by adding a service layer.

Rights are the most common "but they include"

Most platforms bundle perpetual or paid-use rights by default (JoinBrands, Insense, Billo, Trend, Social Cat, HFC, Hummingbirds, minisocial). Benable and TRIBE charge extra for them, and Collabstr sets them per campaign.

Guarantees come in different shapes

Statusphere sells "guaranteed posts," Stack charges only for completed posts, Collabstr holds money in escrow, HFC backfills no-shows, minisocial has a make-good clause. Benable's card on file is the only one that pays back gifted product.

Half of gifted creators never post

Social Cat says 40 to 50% of accepted creators don't deliver and there's no way to recover the product, minisocial's own contract says about 50%, Traackr says 61%. That's the real value of someone chasing and verifying.

Content shops are pulling ahead on data

Billo shows per-creator ROAS, CTR and hook rate against industry medians, and its AI script generator explains itself with real ad data. Nobody on the reach side has anything like it yet, Benable included.
